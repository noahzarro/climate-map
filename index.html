How the values are generated

Source data

Daily gridded observations from E-OBS (ensemble mean, 0.1° grid, v33.0e) for 2011–2025: mean, minimum and maximum temperature and precipitation.

Periods

Every calendar day (e.g. 15 March) is first averaged across all 15 years. Each month is then split into 4 nearly equal parts (48 periods in total; February is treated as 29 days), and the daily averages within each part are averaged again.

The five values

  • Mean / min / max temperature: typical daily mean, minimum and maximum temperature in the period (°C).
  • Precipitation: typical daily precipitation in the period (mm/day), not a total.
  • Wet days per month: the share of days with at least 1 mm of precipitation, scaled to a full month (February = 28.25 days).

Filter

Areas outside the chosen threshold are dimmed. Mean and min temperature hide values below the slider; max temperature, precipitation and wet days hide values above it. The dimmed edges are smoothed for display only.

Note

These are climatological averages over 2011–2025, not forecasts or values for a specific year. Click the map to see all five values at a point.
